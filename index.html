Indian Family Graph

Paste people + relationship data into data.js or the editor, then drag nodes, pan, and zoom.

Data Format

The app now accepts a dedicated data.js structure with separate people and relations arrays.

People fields
id, name, family, optional role
Relation fields
source, target, kind

Supported relation kinds

Spouse, Parent, Child, Sibling, Father, Mother, Son, Daughter

Can I replace the sample data?

Yes. Replace the people and relations arrays in data.js with your own exported records.

Do I need roles for everyone?

No. You can set role only on important anchors like patriarch, matriarch, or family-head and leave it empty for everyone else.

Roles help choose cluster centers, members of the same family are arranged around those centers in circular clusters, marriage links are intentionally longer/weaker than parent links, and familyless records are treated as Other and pushed to outer rings.
